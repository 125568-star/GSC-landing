Dictation App / Version history

Dictation App version history

VersionWhat changed
1.0 — 9 Nov 2026Initial Android app. Dark-themed note and folder screens; record a voice note; transcription attempt; create to-do and shopping lists with checklists; folder management, note colours, import of exported notes, and safeguards around previously saved audio. Early transcription could fail on some Android speech providers.
1.1 — 9 Nov 2026Changed new dictation to Android's live microphone speech-recognition service rather than injecting a pre-recorded PCM stream. Added continuous listening/restart and partial-result handling. Legacy PCM retry remained available but provider-dependent.
1.2 — 10 Nov 2026Moved Unfiled and All Notes to the bottom of the folder list; added automatic saving instead of a Save Text button; black, bordered note editor; centered red REC indicator; title generation and conservative automatic folder classification; stop recording when leaving a note.
1.3 — 10 Nov 2026Added long-press selection of listed notes and multi-select Move/Delete, with confirmation before deletion. Opening notes no longer started recording automatically. Introduced a single manual Record/Stop toggle.
1.4 — 11 Nov 2026Replaced the single toggle with separate tape-recorder-style Record and Stop buttons. Recording starts only when requested. Stopping inserts transcribed text at the saved editor cursor/selection position, rather than always appending at the end. Notes continue to save automatically.
1.5Offline Whisper, WebRTC speech detection, automatic stop after three seconds of silence, and recoverable audio when transcription fails. Adds a Windows dictation helper.
1.5.1Black text on light blue buttons and light note cards. Keeps the editor black and removes unused Android speech recognition code and extra native binaries.
1.5.2Stop cancels Whisper transcription. Record can replace a pending take or redo the most recent transcript in the open editor.
1.5.3Adds a Delete note button in the editor with confirmation.
