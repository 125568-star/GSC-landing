VPNs: Privacy and Your Virtual Location

A VPN encrypts the connection between your device and the VPN server, and websites usually see the server's IP address rather than your home IP address. Selecting a server in another country can make websites think you are visiting from that country.

For example, choosing a server in Kenya or another African country may change the advertisements offered to you. Where advertisers have fewer campaigns targeting that location, some websites may display fewer ads, or different ads. This is not guaranteed: websites can use other location signals, and some serve the same number of adverts everywhere.

A VPN does not automatically block ads or make you anonymous. For more control, review your browser's privacy and ad-blocking settings as well.
