100% Free & Open Source

Every program on this page is free and open-source software. There are no free trials that expire, subscriptions to keep using the programs, or paid licences you must buy. Some projects accept optional donations, but you do not have to pay to use the software.

Image Editing

GIMP lets you edit photos, crop images, remove backgrounds and create graphics without needing an expensive photo-editing subscription.

Word Processing

LibreOffice Writer lets you write letters, resumes, reports and other documents. It is part of LibreOffice, a free office suite that can also work with common Microsoft Office file formats.

Video Editing

Kdenlive lets you cut video clips, add titles and transitions, work with multiple tracks and export finished videos without paying for an editor.

Video Watching & Media Players

VLC media player lets you watch videos and listen to audio in a wide variety of file formats, including MP4, MKV and MP3, without needing extra codec packs.

Audio Editing

Audacity lets you record and edit voice, music and podcasts. You can trim recordings, adjust sound levels and remove unwanted noise.

Screen Recording & Live Streaming

OBS Studio lets you record your computer screen, webcam or tutorials, and broadcast live video when you need to.

Graphic Design & Illustration

Inkscape lets you create logos, diagrams, icons and scalable vector artwork with professional drawing tools.

File Compression

PeaZip lets you compress files, open ZIP and 7z archives, and extract many other archive formats. It is open-source and free for personal or business use, without trial limits.

Web Browsing

Brave is a free, open-source web browser with built-in ad and tracker blocking. You can browse without paying; optional paid services are not required.
